Beginner's Guide to Pickleball

Step 1

Stay behind the baseline of your side of the court when the ball is near you hit it in a upward arc angle the initial hit below your waist

Step 2

Follow guides online, first bounce The receiving team must allow the ball to bounce once on their side before making counter play. Second bounce The serving team must allow the ball to also bounce once on their side before hitting it back.

Step 3

Hit the ball back and forth over the net until a mistake such as out of bounds, hits the net, fails to clear the net, or the ball hits the ground twice on a side.
